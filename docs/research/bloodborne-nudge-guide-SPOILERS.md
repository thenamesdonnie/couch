## 0. Global systems (read first)

### 0.1 Night-phase (moon) system, which kills advance the world

| Phase | Trigger | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Evening | Game start / NG+ start | Real Iosefka behind the clinic door gives free Blood Vials (up to 4 talks). Baseline world. |
| Late evening | Kill Father Gascoigne / first arrival in Cathedral Ward. **[CONFLICT]** Fextralife ties the impostor swap to reaching Cathedral Ward; other guides tie it to killing Blood-starved Beast. | Sky reddens toward sunset. Iosefka's clinic voice becomes the **Impostor Iosefka** (real one is gone for good, no more free vials). Oedon Chapel Dweller can be told about survivors: the **safe-place send system opens**. Window NPCs start asking for a safe place. |
| (world flag, not a moon phase) | Kill **Blood-starved Beast** | **Kidnapper Snatchers** (bag men) spawn in Yharnam/Cathedral Ward. Dying to one = free trip to Hypogean Gaol (Adella, early Darkbeast Paarl, Djura back door). Henryk event definitely active by now. |
| Night | Kill **Vicar Amelia**, then examine the skull on the altar (gives the "Fear the old blood" password memory needed for the Forbidden Woods gate) | Sky goes dark. Church Giants in Cathedral Ward sleep. Window dialogue darkens (madness/beast decline). **Gilbert's Flamesprayer window closes here per Fextralife** [CONFLICT: wikidot lists no explicit deadline before his transformation]. DLC entry item is granted (see §10). Alfred moves to the Forbidden Woods balcony. |
| **Blood Moon** | Kill **Rom, the Vacuous Spider**, then approach the blood-soaked woman in white (Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen apparition) just past the arena | THE hard watershed. See §6 red box. Red moon; Lesser Amygdalae all become visible regardless of Insight; Hunter's Dream gets burnt/flowered look; snatchers gone from streets; Hypogean Gaol lamp breaks; no NPC can be sent to safety any more; window NPCs die or transform; chapel blood offerings (Arianna/Adella) stop; Yahar'gul proper opens. |
| Dawn | Kill **Mergo's Wet Nurse** | "The night, and the dream, were long." Gehrman waits at the great tree for the ending choice. World stays explorable; nothing new is missed by the kill itself. |

Bath Messenger consumable prices rise with each phase advance [minor].

### 0.3 The three Umbilical Cords and endings [ending-relevant]

Player needs **3 consumed** (use from inventory) at any time **before Gehrman is defeated** (mid-fight still counts) for ending 3. Four exist:

| Cord | Source | Deadline / kill-flag |
|---|---|---|
| A | Floor of the **Abandoned Old Workshop** (drop down inside the Healing Church Workshop tower, Cathedral Ward) | Never expires within the playthrough. Only "missed" by never finding it. |
| B | **Impostor Iosefka**: after Rom (Blood Moon) she lies on the clinic operating table; kill her there | **Killed before Rom = cord gone forever** (early kill drops only her Oedon Writhe rune). Available Blood Moon → end of game. |
| C | **Arianna's newborn**: only if Arianna was sent to **Oedon Chapel** and is still alive when **Micolash** dies; she then appears in the tomb/aqueduct below Oedon Chapel with the baby; kill the infant | Kill-flags that destroy it: Arianna sent to Iosefka's clinic; Arianna never sent before Blood Moon; Adella murders her (jealousy, §NPC table); Suspicious Beggar at chapel murders her. |
| D | **Mergo's Wet Nurse** boss drop | Unmissable. |

**Endings** (choice at the great tree after Wet Nurse):
1. **Yharnam Sunrise**, submit to Gehrman. He beheads you; you wake at dawn. Trophy only.
2. **Honoring Wishes**, refuse, kill Gehrman, <3 cords consumed. Moon Presence embraces you; you become the Dream's new keeper (wheelchair scene). First Hunter Badge.
3. **Childhood's Beginning**, refuse, kill Gehrman with 3 cords consumed, then fight and kill the **Moon Presence** (secret true final boss). You become an infant Great One; Doll cradles you. First Hunter Badge.
